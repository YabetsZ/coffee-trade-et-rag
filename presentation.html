The Problem

Ethiopian coffee knowledge is scattered

The challenge
  • Coffee knowledge spans history, farming, brewing, processing, trade, and law
  • Documents are spread across PDFs, articles, and reports
  • Hard to search manually in Amharic
  • No single place to ask questions and get grounded answers

Approach

Why RAG instead of training a model?

Training from scratch requires
  • Massive datasets (millions of examples)
  • Powerful GPU hardware
  • Weeks or months of training time
  • Deep ML expertise
RAG gives us
  • Use powerful pretrained models as-is
  • Connect them to our own documents
  • Update knowledge by adding files with no retraining needed
  • Grounded answers from real sources, not hallucination

Feature Extraction

Semantic embeddings with multilingual-e5-small

What are embeddings?
  • Each text chunk becomes a dense numerical vector
  • Similar meaning produces vectors that are close together
  • Works across languages, Amharic included
  • Normalized vectors: inner product equals cosine similarity
Why not keyword search?
  • Keywords miss synonyms and paraphrases
  • Amharic has many inflected word forms
  • Embeddings capture meaning, not just exact words
  • Better retrieval precision for natural questions
Retrieval

Vector search with FAISS

What FAISS does
  • Stores all 1190 chunk vectors in an index
  • At query time: embed the question, search the index
  • Returns the top-K most similar chunks instantly
  • IndexFlatIP: exact nearest-neighbor search
Generation

Answering with Google Gemini

How the prompt works
  • Retrieved chunks are inserted as context
  • Prompt instructs Gemini to answer only in Amharic
  • Must use only the provided sources
  • Short, natural paragraph with no lists or bullet points
  • State the source file at the end
Why Gemini?
  • State-of-the-art multilingual generation
  • Handles Amharic fluently
  • Follows prompt instructions reliably
  • No local GPU required, fully API-based
  • Fallback to extractive answering if unavailable

Evaluation

Strengths and limitations

Strengths
  • Covers the full breadth of Ethiopian coffee knowledge
  • Amharic-native corpus and answers
  • Grounded answers from real documents
  • Fast retrieval with FAISS
  • Modular, add documents without retraining
  • Clean modern web interface
Limitations
  • Corpus is still relatively small for some topics
  • PDF extraction may lose formatting from scanned files
  • Gemini requires API key and internet connection
